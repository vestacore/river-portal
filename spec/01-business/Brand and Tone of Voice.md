# Brand and Tone of Voice

Back to [[Business Overview]]. Visual system: [[Design Language]].

## Personality
**Clear, warm, open, hopeful.** Like a well-run river crossing: calm, capable and welcoming.

## Voice rules
1. **Plain words first.** "Ask for help", "Give", "Track your gift". River imagery is used in storytelling, never in forms.
2. **Agency, not pity.** "Olena's village keeps its school warm this winter", not "desperate villagers freezing".
3. **We, together.** Credit the chain of people. Never a single hero.
4. **Honest about costs.** "£312 of this campaign paid for diesel and the ferry, and here is the receipt."
5. **Short sentences in help-seeker flows.** Reading age of about 9. See [[Accessibility]].
6. **Never guilt.** No countdown pressure, no shaming, no "only you can save…"
7. **Bilingual parity.** Ukrainian copy is written natively, not machine-translated and left unchecked. See [[Multilingual Experience]].

## Words we use / avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| people who need help, recipients, neighbours | beneficiaries (in public copy), victims, the needy |
| gift, give, share | purchase, buy, sponsor-a-child |
| request, need | case, ticket (in public copy) |
| thank-you, gratitude | testimonial |
| journey of your gift | ROI, conversion |
