# Value for Each Party

Back to [[Business Overview]].

| Party | What they gain | What they give | Protected by |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Recipient]] | Help in the form needed; dignity; control over their story | A clear description of the need; confirmation of receipt (optional thanks) | [[Privacy Model]], [[ADR-005 Open Access for Recipients]] |
| [[Giver]] | Certainty that the gift arrived; a beautiful personal report; thanks | Money, goods, services, time | [[Donor Report]], [[Transparency Ledger]] |
| [[Sponsor]] | Purpose-bound funding with evidence; optional public recognition | Funding for defined costs (often transport) | [[Cost Record]], [[Visibility Levels]] |
| [[Coordinator]] | One calm workspace; less admin; decisions supported by data | Judgement, care, time | [[Coordinator Workspace]], [[Responsibility Matrix]] |
| [[Carrier]] | Clear instructions; costs reimbursed and recorded; recognition | Transport | [[Transport and Logistics Flow]] |
| [[Volunteer]] | Meaningful tasks; visible contribution | Time and skills | [[Recognition]] |
| [[Partner Organisation]] | Shared visibility of needs and offers; less duplication | Capacity, goods, reach | [[Coordination Model]] |
| [[Organisation]] (the charity) | Trust, efficiency, audit-ready records, fundraising power | Operation of the portal | [[Accountability and Audit]], [[Impact Report]] |

## The exchange that is *not* happening
No party pays for visibility, priority or recognition. The river carries gifts, not transactions. See [[Guiding Principles#P1. A gift is a gift]].
