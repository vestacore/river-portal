# Guiding Principles

The non-negotiables. Every feature, screen and data structure is checked against them. The full ethical code is in [[Ethics Charter]]. Back to [[Business Overview]].

## P1. A gift is a gift
A gift is never a purchase. No goods, status or visibility are "bought" with a donation. Givers are thanked for taking part, and amounts are private unless the giver chooses otherwise. → [[Gift]], [[Recognition Anti-Patterns]]

## P2. A need is respected
A need is taken at face value first and verified proportionately. Nobody has to perform suffering to receive help. Needs are never ranked by "worthiness" or displayed for emotional effect. → [[Need]], [[DP-02 Need Verification]]

## P3. The left bank is always open
Anyone may express a need at any time, regardless of history, reputation or verification status. Reputation affects routing and checks. It never affects access. → [[ADR-005 Open Access for Recipients]]

## P4. Private by default
Personal data is visible only to those who need it to deliver help, for as long as they need it. Everything public is aggregated, anonymised or explicitly consented. → [[Privacy Model]], [[ADR-006 Private by Default Visibility]]

## P5. Consent is specific and revocable
Consent covers one purpose (a story, a photo, a name on a thank-you wall) and can be withdrawn. Withdrawal removes the item from publications. → [[Consent]], [[Consent Management]]

## P6. Clear water — clarity and purity of intent
Both sides state their intent simply. Clarity is encouraged and helped along (for example with AI-assisted drafting). It is never demanded as a condition of help. Hidden agendas, such as proselytising, political capture, data harvesting or publicity-for-sale, are declined. → [[Intent Statement]], [[DP-03 Offer Acceptance]]

## P7. The river remembers
Every action is recorded in an append-only log. Corrections are new entries, not edits. This gives us honest reporting and a real audit trail. → [[Log Event]], [[Accountability and Audit]]

## P8. Honest numbers, beautifully shown
Figures shown publicly are derived from the log. Costs, including transport and overheads, are shown openly and not hidden to make impact look bigger. → [[Transparency Ledger]], [[Money Flow and Cost Transparency]]

## P9. Thanks travels upstream
Every hand that took part may receive the recipient's thanks, if the recipient wishes to give it. → [[Gratitude Loop]]

## P10. Dignity in every pixel
The design is warm, bright and positive. It avoids pity imagery, emphasises agency and uses respectful language. → [[Design Language]], [[Brand and Tone of Voice]]

## P11. Scale without changing character
A single-van fundraiser and a multinational programme follow the same principles on the same platform. → [[Scaling Tiers]]
