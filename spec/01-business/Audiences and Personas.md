# Audiences and Personas

Back to [[Business Overview]]. Roles are formalised in [[Role]].

## Primary personas

### Olena, a recipient (uk: Олена)
63, lives in a frontline village and cares for her grandson. She has an old Android phone and uncertain connectivity. She needs a generator and winter medicine. She does not want her face on the internet.
- Needs: a very simple form in Ukrainian, voice input, the option to ask *on behalf of* her neighbours, and SMS updates.
- Fears: being judged, being photographed, forms that ask too much.
- Served by: [[Help Seeker Section]], [[Recipient]], [[Consent]]

### James, an individual giver
41, lives in Leeds and gives monthly. He wants to know that his money became fuel and that the fuel reached someone.
- Needs: one-click giving, a beautiful [[Donor Report]], and a thank-you he can believe.
- Served by: [[Giver Section]], [[Journey Story]], [[Gratitude Loop]]

### Andriy, a coordinator (uk: Андрій)
34, a volunteer turned full-time coordinator. He juggles 40 open requests, three vans and a WhatsApp group.
- Needs: one queue, clear next actions, matching suggestions, cost logging from his phone, and reports that write themselves.
- Served by: [[Coordinator Workspace]], [[Coordination Model]], [[Vertex AI Integration]]

### Mykola, a carrier (uk: Микола)
Drives a volunteer van between Lviv and Kharkiv. He records fuel, tolls and handovers.
- Needs: a mobile checklist per [[Leg]], photo capture at handover, and offline tolerance.
- Served by: [[Carrier]], [[Transport and Logistics Flow]]

### Sarah, sponsor lead at a small company
Funds transport for a quarter. Wants a public report for her company's social value statement and a private one for her board.
- Served by: [[Sponsor]], [[Donor Report]], [[Visibility Levels]]

### Iryna, organisation director / administrator
Accountable to trustees and auditors. Wants oversight without micromanagement.
- Served by: [[Admin Studio]], [[Accountability and Audit]], [[Impact Report]]
