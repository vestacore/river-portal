# Glossary

Canonical terms, their river-metaphor alias and the Ukrainian term used in the UI. The Ukrainian column is the **source of truth for UI translation keys**; see [[Internationalisation]]. Back to [[00 Home]].

| Term (en-GB) | River alias | Ukrainian (uk) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Need]] | Left bank | Потреба | A request for help, expressed by or on behalf of a [[Recipient]]. Always respected, never ranked by worthiness. |
| [[Offer]] | Spring | Пропозиція допомоги | A declared willingness to give: money, goods, services, time or transport. |
| [[Gift]] | Water | Дар | What is actually given once an Offer is accepted. A gift is never sold, exchanged or made conditional on publicity. |
| [[Flow]] | Stream | Потік | The living link between one or more Gifts and one or more Needs. The central unit of coordination. |
| [[Consignment]] | Boat | Відправлення | A physical bundle of [[Item]]s moving towards a Need. |
| [[Leg]] | Reach | Етап доставки | One segment of transport, from one [[Location]] or [[Hub]] to another, by one [[Carrier]]. |
| [[Hub]] | Pool | Хаб / склад | A place where goods rest, are sorted or re-packed. |
| [[Cost Record]] | Toll | Запис витрат | A recorded cost (transport, fuel, customs, packaging, postage), always attributed to a Flow or Leg. |
| [[Delivery Confirmation]] | Mouth of the river | Підтвердження отримання | The Recipient's (or proxy's) confirmation, with optional photos and notes. |
| [[Gratitude Note]] | Returning tide | Подяка | A message of thanks travelling back upstream to everyone who took part. |
| [[Reputation]] | The Current | Репутація | The accumulated, explainable history of how someone has taken part in flows. Never a gate to help for those in need. |
| [[Intent Statement]] | Source | Намір | A short declaration of why someone is asking or giving. Used for clarity, not judgement. |
| [[Verification]] | Soundings | Перевірка | A check that a claim is real (identity, need, organisation, delivery). Proportionate to risk. |
| [[Consent]] | — | Згода | A recorded, revocable permission for a specific use of personal data or story. |
| [[Visibility Policy]] | Clear / murky water | Налаштування видимості | Who may see what, per field and per audience. Private by default. |
| [[Coordinator]] | Riverkeeper | Координатор | A person who shapes flows: triage, matching, routing, reporting. Many may work on one flow. |
| [[Carrier]] | Boatman | Перевізник | Anyone who moves goods: volunteer driver, courier, postal service, logistics company. |
| [[Giver]] | Spring | Благодійник / дарувальник | A person or organisation that gives. |
| [[Sponsor]] | Tributary | Спонсор | A giver who funds a defined purpose, often costs such as transport. |
| [[Recipient]] | Left bank | Отримувач | A person, family, community or institution with a Need. |
| [[Campaign]] | Channel | Кампанія / збір | A time-bound, purpose-bound collection (e.g. "Fuel for three convoys"). |
| [[Programme]] | Basin | Програма | A long-running body of work containing many campaigns and flows. |
| [[Log Event]] | Sediment | Подія журналу | An immutable, append-only record of something that happened. |
| [[Projection]] | Surface | Проєкція | A read model derived from Log Events (dashboards, public pages, reports). |
| [[Journey Story]] | River journey | Історія шляху | A publication tracing a gift from source to mouth, with consented detail only. |
| [[Donor Report]] | — | Звіт для донора | A beautiful, per-giver report; private by default, publishable by choice. |
| [[Transparency Ledger]] | — | Реєстр прозорості | The public, aggregated record of income, costs and deliveries. |
