# The River Concept

The portal is modelled as **a river bed**. It does not push aid at people or sell good deeds. It gives help a channel so that it can reach where it is needed, in the form in which it is needed. Back to [[Business Overview]].

## The parts of the river

| River | System | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Left bank** | Those in need and their expressed needs | [[Recipient]], [[Need]] |
| **Right bank** | Those willing to share, and what they offer | [[Giver]], [[Sponsor]], [[Offer]] |
| **Springs and tributaries** | Sources of gifts: individuals, sponsors, partner organisations | [[Gift]], [[Partner Organisation]] |
| **The stream** | A living link between gifts and needs | [[Flow]] |
| **Channels and basins** | Purposeful groupings of streams | [[Campaign]], [[Programme]] |
| **Boats and reaches** | Physical movement of goods | [[Consignment]], [[Leg]], [[Carrier]] |
| **Pools** | Places where goods rest and are sorted | [[Hub]] |
| **Tolls** | The real cost of moving water | [[Cost Record]] |
| **Riverkeepers** | People who shape and tend the channel | [[Coordinator]] |
| **The mouth** | Arrival and confirmation | [[Delivery Confirmation]] |
| **The returning tide** | Thanks flowing back to every hand | [[Gratitude Note]], [[Gratitude Loop]] |

## What the metaphor commits us to

1. **Water finds its level.** Help goes to where the need is. Matching is driven by the need's shape (what, where, when, in what form), not by the giver's convenience alone. See [[DP-04 Matching]].
2. **Banks are distinct but connected.** Recipients and givers do not have to meet or know each other's identities. The river connects them, and [[Coordinator]]s tend it. See [[Privacy Model]].
3. **Form matters.** Water arrives as it is needed: cash for a pharmacy, a generator rather than money for one, a lift rather than a bus fare. [[Need]] captures the *form* as well as the *what*.
4. **The river remembers.** Sediment is never removed. Every action is appended to the log and never overwritten. See [[ADR-001 Event-Sourced Append Log]].
5. **The surface shows the depths honestly.** Public pages are projections of real records and never hand-written claims. See [[Transparency Ledger]].
6. **The current carries trust.** Reputation is the accumulated flow of real participation. It helps coordinators decide *how* to route and verify. It never closes the left bank. See [[Reputation Dynamics]].
7. **Clear water.** Clarity of the request, honesty of the offer and purity of intent on both sides are noticed, valued and made visible to those who need to see them. They are never used to shame anyone. See [[Intent Statement]].

## Where the metaphor stops

The metaphor guides structure and tone. It must never obscure plain language in the UI. A recipient sees "Your request", not "Your left bank". River names appear in brand storytelling and in this vault's aliases. See [[Brand and Tone of Voice]].
