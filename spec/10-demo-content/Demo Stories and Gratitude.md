# Demo Stories and Gratitude

Journey stories and gratitude notes for the demo scenarios, in en-GB and Ukrainian. Every item states its visibility and the consent that allows it. Tone follows [[Brand and Tone of Voice]]: agency, not pity; the chain of people, not a single hero. Back to [[Demo Content Overview]].

> [!principle] Thanks travels upstream
> A thank-you is the recipient's gift back. It is invited, never required, and the recipient chooses who sees it and how they are named. — [[Guiding Principles#P9. Thanks travels upstream]]

## Journey stories

### Story 1 — "Light for homework" (Scenario A)

Visibility: `public` · Consent: Olena `story.public` (first name, oblast, "grandmother"; no photo of her or her home), carriers Tom, Priya and Mykola `name.public` (first name). Approved at [[DP-10 Report Publication]] on 6 March 2026 and released publicly on 12 March 2026, after the 14-day conflict-zone delay ([[Canonical Parameters]], [[Visibility Levels#Redaction rules per projection]]); the `participants` version was available from 3 March.

| en-GB | uk |
|---|---|
| **Light for homework** | **Світло для уроків** |
| In February, 41 people in the UK gave towards one van journey. In Leeds, volunteers packed two generators, blankets, hygiene kits and power banks. | У лютому 41 людина у Великій Британії долучилася до однієї поїздки бусом. У Лідсі волонтери спакували два генератори, ковдри, гігієнічні набори й павербанки. |
| Tom and Priya drove for three days, across the North Sea and five countries, to our hub in Lviv. Andriy counted every box: 114 of 114. | Том і Прія три дні їхали — через Північне море та п'ять країн — до нашого хабу у Львові. Андрій перерахував кожну коробку: 114 із 114. |
| The next morning Mykola set off east. By lunchtime on 25 February, Olena — who looks after her grandson in a village in Kharkiv oblast — had a generator by her door. | Наступного ранку Микола вирушив на схід. До обіду 25 лютого Олена, яка доглядає онука в селі на Харківщині, вже мала генератор біля своїх дверей. |
| That evening the lights stayed on during the outage. Her medicine stayed cold, and her grandson finished his homework. | Того вечора, попри відключення, світло не згасло. Ліки залишилися в холоді, а онук доробив уроки. |
| *Journey: Leeds → Harwich → Hook of Holland → Lviv → Kharkiv oblast · 2 carriers' legs · costs GBP 2,012.43 for the whole van, shown in the ledger.* | *Шлях: Лідс → Гарідж → Гук-ван-Голланд → Львів → Харківщина · 2 етапи доставки · витрати на весь бус — 2 012,43 GBP, усе в реєстрі прозорості.* |

**Participants-only version** (default, before story consent): "A grandmother caring for her grandson in Kharkiv oblast received a generator on 25 February. Thank you for being part of this journey." / «Бабуся, яка доглядає онука на Харківщині, отримала генератор 25 лютого. Дякуємо, що були частиною цього шляху.»

### Story 2 — "Clean water, three legs" (Scenario B)

Visibility: `public` · Consent: hospital `name.public` (institution only); Kryla `name.public` (organisation); Mykola (first name). No patients or staff are named.

| en-GB | uk |
|---|---|
| **Clean water, three legs** | **Чиста вода в три етапи** |

### Story 3 — "The library that stays open" (Scenario C)

Visibility: `public` · Consent: warm-space manager for the institution; photo of the library reading room with no people, EXIF stripped. Translated from Ukrainian with AI assistance and reviewed ([[Scenario C — Humanitarian Programme#AI-assisted translation — worked example]]).

| uk (original) | en-GB |
|---|---|
| **Бібліотека, яка не зачиняється** | **The library that stays open** |
| Коли в селі зникає світло, люди йдуть до старої бібліотеки. Тут тепло, можна зарядити телефон, випити чаю й просто побути разом. | When the power goes in the village, people head to the old library. It is warm, you can charge your phone, have a cup of tea and simply be together. |
| Восени завдяки програмі «Тепла зима» тут з'явилася зарядна станція із сонячними панелями. Тепер бібліотека працює навіть під час найдовших відключень. | This autumn, through the Warm Winter programme, the library received a power station with solar panels. Now it stays open through even the longest outages. |
| «Наші читачі тепер приходять не лише по книжки», — усміхається завідувачка. | "Our readers don't only come for books any more," the librarian says with a smile. |

## Moderation rules applied to demo content

- Free text PII-scrubbed: GN-0205 originally named the street; replaced before the event was appended ([[Privacy Model#Separation of PII from events]]).
- No note mentions a child by name or age in public copy (GN-0143 counts pupils only).
- Gratitude is never solicited as a condition, and absence of thanks is never shown ("3 of 5 recipients said thank you" is **not** displayed). See [[Recognition Anti-Patterns]].
