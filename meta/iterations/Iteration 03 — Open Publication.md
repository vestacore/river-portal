## Goal
Prepare the repository for GitHub:
- a clear and attractive README with screenshots;
- a disclaimer: this is a research project, a free template, and dedicated to Oleshky;
- a sourced fact sheet on the blockade of Oleshky;
- licences (ADR-0019), contribution and security guidance.

## Log
- 2026-09-25: Licensing resolved: Apache 2.0 instead of the "MIT" mentioned in passing; CC BY-NC 4.0 instead of CC BY 4.0, because the owner wants commercial use by agreement only.
- 2026-09-25: Screenshots taken from the **production** build on the public surface (no staff controls) with `tools/design-shot.mjs`. The key images stay full colour, because palette compression turned a small teal accent grey.
- 2026-09-25: Oleshky fact sheet researched by a research agent from 30 dated sources. The editor re-checked the key claims against the UN human rights mission (25 June), the Kyiv Independent (17 September), AFP (21 September) and the BBC (24 September).
- 2026-09-25: Wording decision. No official or international body has yet called Oleshky *genocide*. The README therefore states "genocide" as the **authors' position**, clearly labelled, and the fact sheet records exactly which terms officials and bodies have used (terrorism, a possible war crime of using human shields, a Holodomor parallel). This keeps the advocacy honest and hard to discredit.
